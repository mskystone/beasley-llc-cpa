Welcome to my practice!

My name is David Beasley. My goal is to become your most trusted personal and/or business financial advisor. As a certified public accountant with over 30 years experience I offer multiple services that address the financial needs of both individuals and businesses.

My success is measured by the success of my clients. I am committed to provide you with the highest quality tax, accounting and consulting services on a timely and professional basis. My goals as a trusted financial advisor is to be available when you need me, responsive to your needs and provide insightful advice to enable you to make informed financial decisions.

I offer a variety of services focused on individuals and businesses, please feel free to give me call for a free consultation.

For individuals I offer tax planning services, tax preparation, estate planning and personal finance services.

About Me

R. David Beasley, Jr

I am a certified public accountant with over 30 years experience. I began my career in public accounting in 1991 and worked with KPMG and PwC. After 8 years in public accounting, I went into industry and worked in several upper level management positions within the retail and distribution, communications, software and hardware and pharmaceutical industries.

Through out my career I have developed a keen business acumen that allows me to use the language of accounting and tax to convey opportunities to improve financial results and maximize tax savings. My passion is delivering focused personalized attention to both individuals and businesses and helping them establish, monitor and accomplish their financial objectives.

I have lived and worked in the Atlanta area since 1979 and the Towne Lake Area since March 2000. I am married and have three children whom I also coach during the football, baseball and softball seasons and I am an active member of my Church.

I graduated from Kennesaw State University with a Bachelors of Business Administration in Accounting and Finance in 1991. I am a member of the Georgia Society of CPA’s and the American Institute of CPA’s. I received my license to practice in the state of Georgia in 1999 - license #CPA019915.

Accounting & Assurance Services

01

Bookkeeping and Record Maintenance

Prepare and maintain of supporting documents to record monthly transactions, reconcile bank accounts, manage accounts receivable and payable, maintain fixed assets records and provide management financial statements periodically.

02

Payroll

Prepare and process payroll, generate checks, prepare quarterly and annual filings and prepare and distribute employee W-2’s and contractor/vendor 1099’s.

03

Personal Financial Statements

Prepare financial statements based upon personal information provided.

04

Compilation

Prepare financial statements from information provided by clients, no assurance provided.

05

Reviews

Inquiry and analytical procedures, which provides limited assurance on financial statements.

06

Audits

An intensive examination providing the highest level of assurance on financial statements.

Home / Services / Controllership Services

Controllership Services

01

Contract CFO/Controller

Temporary or project-by-project basis assistance for companies in a role transition or periodic basis for small or start-up companies that can not afford a full time CFO/Controller.

02

Public Reporting

Temporary or periodic assistance with the preparation and filing of SEC documents required by public companies. Preparation of S-1, S-4, 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, Form 4, Proxy Statements and earnings releases.

03

Policies and Procedures

Establish and document formal accounting and finance policies and procedures to create accounting consistency and accuracy.

04

Sarbanes-Oxley Compliance

Provide guidance and support establishing a complete set of SOX documents or work with existing documentation to compile a set of documentation.

05

Internal Audit

Assist with establishing an internal audit department or provide periodic internal audit services to companies that lack the size to justify an internal audit department.

06

Special Investigations

Use forensic auditing procedures to prove or disprove claims or allegations filed by or against a company or individual.

07

Income Tax Provisions

Preparation of periodic corporate tax provisions to record an income tax provision in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles.
